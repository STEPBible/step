STEPBible Introduction and study guides

This YouTube channel introduces you to the basic use, and leads you into deeper study. You'll find yourself looking into the original text without needing to know any Hebrew or Greek, and you'll find out how to delve into the text for yourself.

Finding your way round STEPBible

How to do the simple things: Pick your Bible language and version, find words or phrases, compare versions, look up the original (without knowing Greek or Hebrew) or quickly prepare a Bible study by looking up a topic. View this video on YouTube here.

Using STEPBible in your own language

How to get help and menus in French, or Arabic or Chinese or 50 other languages. There are Bibles in about 300 languages, and each of them links you to searches for the original vocabulary without needing to know Hebrew or Greek. If your language isn't there, you can add a new skin - this video shows you how.  View this video on YouTube here.

Using STEPBible without any internet

How to download the complete STEPBible tool and select from hundreds of Bibles and commentaries. This works in a browser on a PC or Mac as if you were online. It is free and legal to give away to your friends. View this video on YouTube here.

Finding a Hebrew word without knowing Hebrew

This video shows how to find all the places in Ruth chapter 1 where the Hebrew verb shub ('turn / return') occurs, even if you know no Hebrew at all. You don't need to look for Strong's Numbers or any other arcane tricks. View this video on YouTube here.

A STEPBible Study: Grace in John

See how to do an in-depth study by looking at "grace and truth" in John.1.16. This small phrase stands out as a key to the gospel, and John expects us to know the Old Testament in enough detail to understand the prophecy he is pointing to. With STEPBible the solution is easy.  View this video on YouTube here.

A STEPBible Study: Days and Generations in Genesis

Finding your way round a large book like Genesis is easy in STEPBible. Follow the clues and find the structure based on "generations". Investigate the meaning of the "days" in Genesis 1. Find out how to investigate the exact meanings without knowing Hebrew or Greek. View this video on YouTube here,

A STEPBible Study: The Women in Matthew 1

Four women are listed in the genealogy of Jesus. Using STEP Bible it is easy to find the significance and story behind them. By turning on colour-coding for grammar, you can even feel the same shock that the original readers experienced when they read about Mary, without knowing any Greek yourself. View this video on YouTube here.

A STEPBible Study: The Controversial Beginning and End of Mark 1

Did Jesus feel "compassion" or "indignation" when he met the leper in Mark 1? STEP Bible can help to explain the different translations. Are the last verses of Mark 16 as ancient as the rest of the gospel? Look at the fact and decide for yourself. Find out how to delve into tricky issues using STEPBible.
