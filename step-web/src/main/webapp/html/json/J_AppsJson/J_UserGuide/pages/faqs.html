STEP Bible software is developed by an international team centered in Cambridge UK
We welcome your questions and feedback.  For best results, please use the Feedback form in STEPBible to report issues.  At the place in STEPBible that you are experiencing a problem, invoke the Feedback and contact  window from the Help dropdown (three vertical dots at the top right corner of the STEPBible display). STEPBible will display a contact form which, when you submit it, will include a screenshot of the problem area along with your message.
