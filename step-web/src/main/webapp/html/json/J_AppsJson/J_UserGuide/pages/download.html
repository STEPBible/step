Download STEPBible for Offline Use

You can download STEPBible from the internet, or install it from a USB or SD card, into any Windows PC, Macintosh, or Linux computer. You can download Android and iOS apps from their respective app stores.

To download STEPBible to your computer, click on the Help button (three vertically stacked dots) at the top right corner of the STEPBible display and choose Download STEPBible.
